Application timeline

Best time to apply for study in the EU

Month 1-3

Research and shortlisting

Choose your course, target country, and shortlist universities. This is the ideal period to compare tuition, scholarship options, and admission cycles.

Month 3-6

Application and document prep

Prepare transcripts, SOP, recommendation letters, passport, English proficiency, and financial support documents. Apply early for stronger chances.

Month 6-9

Offer and scholarship follow-up

Track applications, respond to admissions teams, and secure your university offer before finalizing visa and travel documents.

Month 9-12

Visa, accommodation, and travel

Submit your visa, book your accommodation, prepare your travel insurance, and attend pre-departure coaching with your dedicated agent.
